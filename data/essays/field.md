# The Shape of the Essay Field

June 2025  
  
An essay has to tell people something they don't already know. But there are three different reasons people might not know something, and they yield three very different kinds of essays.  
  
One reason people won't know something is if it's not important to know. That doesn't mean it will make a bad essay. For example, you might write a good essay about a particular model of car. Readers would learn something from it. It would add to their picture of the world. For a handful of readers it might even spur some kind of epiphany. But unless this is a very unusual car it's not critical for everyone to know about it. \[[1](#f1n)
