# Naval Ravikant

**Naval Ravikant** (born November 5, 1974) is an Indian-born American entrepreneur and investor. He is the co-founder and chairman of [AngelList](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AngelList "AngelList"), a platform for startups, investors, and job seekers.[^2] He is an angel investor who has made early-stage investments in companies including [Uber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uber "Uber"), [Twitter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twitter "Twitter"), [Postmates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postmates "Postmates"), and [Yammer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yammer "Yammer").[^3] Ravikant is a recipient of the [Edmund Hillary Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edmund_Hillary_Fellowship "Edmund Hillary Fellowship").[^4] He also co-hosts a podcast with Brett Hall.[^5]

## Early life and education

Ravikant was born in [New Delhi, India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Delhi "New Delhi"), in 1974. He moved to New York City with his mother and his brother, Kamal, when he was 9 years old. He attended [Stuyvesant High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stuyvesant_High_School "Stuyvesant High School") and graduated in 1991.[^6] In 1995, he graduated from [Dartmouth College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dartmouth_College "Dartmouth College") with degrees in [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science "Computer science") and economics.[^6] During college, he interned at the law firm [Davis Polk & Wardwell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Davis_Polk_%26_Wardwell "Davis Polk & Wardwell").[^6]

## Career

After graduating, Ravikant briefly worked at [Boston Consulting Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston_Consulting_Group "Boston Consulting Group") before moving to [Silicon Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silicon_Valley "Silicon Valley").[^6]

### Epinions

In 1999, Ravikant co-founded the consumer product review site [Epinions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epinions "Epinions"). The company raised $45 million in venture capital from firms including [Benchmark Capital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benchmark_Capital "Benchmark Capital") and [August Capital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/August_Capital "August Capital").[^7] In 2003, Epinions merged with the comparison pricing site [Dealtime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dealtime "Dealtime") to become [Shopping.com](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shopping.com "Shopping.com"), which held an [Initial public offering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Initial_public_offering "Initial public offering") (IPO) in October 2004.[^7]

In January 2005, Ravikant and three of his co-founders filed a lawsuit against Benchmark Capital, August Capital, and co-founder Nirav Tolia, alleging they were misled into approving the merger under the pretense that the company was worth less than the capital raised, making their shares worthless.[^7] The lawsuit was settled in December 2005.[^8]

### Hit Forge

Around 2007, Ravikant started a $20 million early-stage venture capital fund named *The Hit Forge*. The fund invested in startups including [Twitter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twitter "Twitter"), [Uber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uber "Uber"), and [Stack Overflow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stack_Overflow "Stack Overflow").[^9]

### AngelList

In 2007, Ravikant began co-writing a blog called *Venture Hacks*, which offered detailed advice on negotiating term sheets.[^10] That blog evolved into [AngelList](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AngelList "AngelList"), which Ravikant co-founded in 2010 as a platform for startups to connect with angel investors.[^10] AngelList also operates the product discovery platform [Product Hunt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Product_Hunt "Product Hunt"). In 2022, AngelList Venture raised funds at a $4 billion valuation.[^11]

### MetaStable Capital

![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/93/Naval_Ravikant_in_2011_%28cropped%291.jpg/250px-Naval_Ravikant_in_2011_%28cropped%291.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)

Ravikant in 2011

In 2014, Ravikant co-founded MetaStable Capital, a [cryptocurrency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryptocurrency "Cryptocurrency") [hedge fund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hedge_fund "Hedge fund").[^12] A June 2017 regulatory filing reported the fund's assets as $69 million, with investors including [Andreessen Horowitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andreessen_Horowitz "Andreessen Horowitz") and [Sequoia Capital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sequoia_Capital "Sequoia Capital").[^12]

### Spearhead.co investment fund

In 2017, Ravikant launched Spearhead, an investment fund that provides capital to founders to enable them to invest in technology startups as angel investors.[^13] The fund's third cohort raised $100 million for 15 founders.[^3] Companies backed by Spearhead founders include [Neuralink](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neuralink "Neuralink"), [Opendoor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Opendoor "Opendoor"), and [Rippling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rippling "Rippling").[^14]

### Podcasting and writing

Ravikant hosts a podcast, Nav.al, where he discusses topics including philosophy and investing. He has also been a guest on podcasts such as *[The Joe Rogan Experience](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Joe_Rogan_Experience "The Joe Rogan Experience")* and *[The Tim Ferriss Show](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Tim_Ferriss_Show "The Tim Ferriss Show")*.[^15]

With Ravikant's permission, Eric Jorgenson curated his tweets, essays, and interviews into a book titled *The Almanack of Naval Ravikant: A Guide to Wealth and Happiness*.[^16]

### Airchat

In 2023, Ravikant co-founded Airchat, a social media application that uses voice notes and AI-powered transcription.[^17]
