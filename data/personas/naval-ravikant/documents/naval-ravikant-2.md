# Naval Ravikant

## About

He co-founded a platform called Angel List which helped startups raise money directly from Angel investors. He's himself invested in some of the most successful tech companies including Twitter, Uber, and Postmates.

## Before fame

In 1999 during the dot-com boom, he co-founded consumer review website Epinions. The company would later merge with another company before going public in 2004.

## Trivia

He's gained widespread popularity via Twitter where he has over 2 million followers. He Tweets about a wide variety of topics including meditation, wisdom, personal finance, and other more general life tips,

## Family life

He moved from India to New York with his mother, father, and brother at the age of 9.

## Associated with

In 2015 he was interviewed by [Tim Ferriss](https://www.famousbirthdays.com/people/timothy-ferriss.html). He's also given other memorable interviews including one with [Joe Rogan](https://www.famousbirthdays.com/people/joe-rogan.html) in 2019.
